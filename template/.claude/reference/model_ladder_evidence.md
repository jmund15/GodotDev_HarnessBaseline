# Model Ladder

The model table. Load it whenever you pin a model or an effort.

- **Owners elsewhere:** dispatch rules are `orchestration` §5; tier claims are registry `roles`; availability is `reference/external_models.json` (`python3 .claude/tools/model_registry.py available`); launching a sidecar is `reference/sidecar_dispatch.md`. An unavailable model is out: re-select from what remains.
- **`evidence`** tags each row's sources: `pub` published vendor or independent results, `bench` our battery, `use` owner rulings and real use. No source gates a pin.
- **Rows name product-line families.** A row covers every registry row of its family on any transport, and `scout pool` covers each family it lists. Dispatch a model no row covers only by the alias the user names, never as a substitute chosen by rule.
- **Detail behind a row** is in the Obsidian vault: `bench` on `Claude/Meta/Benchmark/model-effort-v1.0/RESULTS-BOARD.md` (`TASKS.md` beside it maps each work shape to its task), `pub` in `Claude/Meta/Benchmark/ladder-ingest/pub-evidence.md`. The table settles every routine pick; read the detail only for a question about the ladder itself (a measurement, an ingest, an audit of a cell).

## Role definitions

Five tiers, named by work shape. `python3 .claude/tools/model_registry.py for-role <tier>` prints the rows whose registry `roles` explicitly claim that tier on this seat and across the hop. A stronger row does not automatically claim lower tiers; use its measured `±` cell before choosing it for a different work shape.

A tier a row lists in registry `supplementary` and not in `roles` is for review seats only. That seat sits beside qualified seats (the tier in `roles`, not in `supplementary`) that together carry all of its mandates, and it covers no mandate alone. A row that lists the tier in both also authors at it, and the review ledger counts its seat there only beside a qualified one. `for-role` and `provider_bands.py lean` list such a row apart from the claimants.

| tier | work shape |
|---|---|
| `orchestrator` | scoping, dispatching, reviewing across systems; long-horizon sessions; the final cross-system design verdict |
| `architect` | solution design at any scope: fresh and scoped architecting, planning from a thin or thick design, design and plan review |
| `executor` | implementation and verification under a scope: coding, root-cause debugging, fix authorship, red-team of a concrete artifact |
| `fanout` | read-heavy and mechanical work: surveys, enumeration, text comparison, rubric checks, tight-spec execution |
| `scout` | locate / enumerate / extract, verifiable without applying project doctrine |

`validation` is not a tier: it is the `fanout` row at a lower effort pin.

Terms the cells use:

- **fresh architecting:** design from a brief with no existing design to extend. **Scoped architecting:** one change inside an existing system.
- **thin-spec planning:** the design leaves choices open and the planner settles them. **Thick-spec planning:** the design is settled and the plan sequences and covers it.
- **tight-spec execution:** the spec states every behavior. **Loose-spec execution:** it states the goal and leaves the means. **Converged work:** the plan is settled and only execution remains. **Ambiguous execution:** the executor must judge a response no check scores (`orchestration` reference `slice_pins.md`).
- **oracle:** a mechanical check that scores the result, such as a test suite or a golden file. **Parity refactor:** a restructure that keeps behavior identical, proven by that check.
- **chained research:** each answer is the input to the next question. **Lens:** one narrow review or audit pass with a stated check; an **anchored** lens is handed the artifact and the check.

## Role guidance

Roles are relative to the session's model. `±` = whatever decides a pin within the row's claimed tiers, `+` for the row and `−` against it: a strength, an efficiency or inefficiency, a weakness or a reliability limit, for example. Judge a work shape with no cell from the row's intel and its nearby shapes; never bar it. An effort range names its ends: start at the lowest rung and rise per `orchestration` §5 *Effort*.

- **You choose every pin; no tool chooses it.** Tools drop only rows that are wrong in every case (no tier claim, unavailable, a plan refused on capacity). No listing order or advisory is a pick. The one pin a tool fills is an engine's own checked step (`orchestration` §5 *Effort*). Weigh the rest in one pass:
  1. **Fit sets the floor; the job's bar sets how much quality to buy above it.** A row clears the floor when its registry `roles` claim the tier; its `±` cell says how it fares there, and the bar decides what that is worth. The owner's stated preference (cost-effective, or maximum quality), the owner's lean condition (`orchestration` reference `spend_lean.md`) or the work's rigor sets the bar. With neither stated, quality leads where it decides the result (architecting, design, review) and cost leads elsewhere (execution under a settled spec, fan-out, extraction). Where rows match on the shape, a stronger row buys nothing. A board lead inside the judge band is no quality difference.
  2. **Among rows that meet the bar, cost picks the row and room picks its plan.** Cost is the row's `cost` cell. Room is the paying plan's quota left against its window left (the `[budget-posture]` line). Room never justifies a costlier row on the same plan. It trades against cost only across plans: a cheap row on a plan spending faster than its window can lose to a pricier row on a plan with room. The same row on two plans goes to the one with more room. Speed breaks ties.
- **Review seats:** step 1's floor applies, and a row holding the tier only as `supplementary` clears it for an added seat beside a qualified one (§Role definitions). Among rows that clear it, prefer a provider other than the subject's author when that provider's plan has room. The preference never adds a seat.
- **Session vs delegate:** at equal intel the lower-`deleg` row is the better delegate. Pair an orchestrator-row session with architect- and executor-row delegates.
- **`agentType: 'Explore'`** composes with any model pin and loads no project doctrine, cutting first-turn input by about a third. Use it only for read-only locate/enumerate/extract lenses, never one that must apply project rules.
- **Compaction:** a pin's compaction point (`model_registry.py usable_window`) bounds a job's whole working set: the subject it must keep resident, the reads it makes and its reasoning, not only its required reading. A lane of independent items (pages to extract, files to scan) compacts and carries on. A job that must keep one subject resident (a review seat, a design, a plan) and outgrows the point can lose the subject at each compaction, re-read it and cycle. Give such a job a narrower mandate or a smaller subject, or a row or route whose point holds it. A proxied agent compacts only on an adapter build that detects the client's compaction control.

## Axis definitions

Higher = better on every axis. `?` = no evidence has set the axis yet.

- **intel:** the hardest problem handled unsupervised.
- **deleg:** orchestration ability (scoping, spec-writing, dispatching, reviewing returns, holding a long plan); it decides who runs a session. `—` = never a session model.
- **taste:** UI/UX, code cleanliness, API design and prose, relative to the owner's preference.
- **speed:** time to completion.
- **cost:** what one result costs at the row's price (registry `price`). On a plan row it weighs quota draw, never spend.
- **billing:** vendor · how a call is paid: `plan` spends a subscription quota, `pay-as-you-go` spends money per token, `free` spends neither.
- **effort:** the start rung per work shape, with the signal that moves it up or down: where an effort pin starts for judgment-stage `agent()` dispatches and the session `/effort`. A rung is named only here. A result at one rung shows that rung is enough, not that the others fail. A rung a row's registry `effort.avoid` lists is not pinned except inside an observable comparison: a `sidecar_fanout.py --compare` run or a benchmark campaign file, never a measurement the dispatcher declares in text; `effort.avoidRef` cites the evidence, and the guards and launchers read it from the registry. A rung joins `effort.avoid` only when it has no valid use: on every plan that serves the row, another rung does the same work as well for less. A rung that is still the best pick somewhere, such as a backup on a plan with room when the others run short, stays off the list. `—` = no effort knob.

Harness load is a third pin beside model and effort: `orchestration` §5 *Per-dispatch harness cost*.
