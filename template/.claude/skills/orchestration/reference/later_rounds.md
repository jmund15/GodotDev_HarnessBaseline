# Later review rounds

Read before seating a review of a subject an earlier round reviewed (`orchestration` §2 *Later rounds* points here).

Rigor sets which findings owe a round: a unit that answers only a finding below the run's bar owes no re-check ([`rigor.md`](rigor.md)); the other triggers in this file stand, and every later-round seat is told the same bar.

A later round reviews only the changes whose blast radius no reviewer has covered: a revision that adds or widens an effect on a decision, contract, consumer or outcome no reviewer judged. A new type, mechanism, rule, contract or plan decision is the usual case. The criterion holds at any rigor, whoever wrote or proposed the change, because no reviewer judged that effect: a reviewer's suggested fix was judged as a remedy for its finding, never for what it does to the rest of the subject. Whoever rules the revision judges the criterion; no list of plan lines, doctrine kinds or authors decides it. A revision whose effect stays inside a unit a reviewer already judged, or narrows it (a ruling, a cut, a correction confined to its finding's unit), the session reads against its finding and records (`review_ledger.py record --inline <who> --mandate <m>`). A verified literal text finding the fold transcribed exactly as its `old`/`new` pair states records done (`post_check.py` records it) while its effect stays inside its finding's unit. The widest case is a rewrite of the core design, a decision most unchanged units depend on: it gets the full panel again with `--ignore-ledger` on `plan`. Rounds end because each revision reaches less unreviewed ground, never by a count.

The measure is the actual revised diff, which the session reads: `git diff <reviewed head>..HEAD`, plus the consumers `findReferences` reaches from changed symbols for code, and `review_ledger.py uncovered` and `plan`, which name the pairs the revised bytes leave without coverage. The post-check proposes no round; the session states which units the criterion reached and why. A seat's width follows how many units the criterion reached, their kind and how widely they are cited; its depth follows how deep their defects can hide. A later-round seat checks the units the criterion reached against the mandates whose findings cite them (for a delegate's `choices`, the command's design mandates, [`writer_returns.md`](writer_returns.md)), plus grounding and coherence.

Whatever builds a round's jobs scopes them to the changed units by default. A round builder is a surface that, after a fix, creates the next check's jobs from a subject or a jobs file: review fan-outs, judge panels, visual and runtime checks, proof reruns. Its next round's must-read is the fix's diff against the last reviewed version plus the direct citers a tool lists for the changed units (`citers.py` for doctrine; for code no builder lists them, so the session adds the consumers `findReferences` reaches); the whole subject stays context. The session may add units under the criterion above; the builder never decides blast radius. A whole-subject round runs only for an uncovered mandate or the base walk.

A mandate left uncovered or failed re-runs over the whole subject. A supported critical inside a remedy a fold already applied gets the smallest remedy the existing design carries, checked against the original finding and the remedy's citers. A unit stops converging when a counting finding on it comes back after its fix (the same defect again, or the same aim failed a new way, such as one owner rule or mandate traded for another), or when only added design (a new type, mechanism, rule or contract) would answer a counting finding on it. It gets no further fix round, and neither a narrower patch nor a pick between variants of the failed design is its exit: it returns to the stage that designed it, for candidate approaches drawn from the aim behind its findings, the frame the failed design assumed included. A candidate set that is a taste fork goes to the owner as a design decision; a dead end halts as non-convergence (drive halt H5). Other units keep converging meanwhile. A review whose subject the owner judges by sight stops sooner: the project's UI skill caps its fix rounds, and a finding still standing goes to the owner on the page. A later-round seat is a clean context: it receives the revised subject and never an earlier round's findings or ledger, which would steer a seat meant to judge fresh. The revised subject carries each disposition's rationale inline, and the orchestrator dedups the seat's findings against its own earlier-round ledger and escalates only a new finding or one the inline rationale does not answer. A fact check is not a round. A unit the plan states and the delegate transcribed gets correctness checks (tests, diff read), not a second design review.
