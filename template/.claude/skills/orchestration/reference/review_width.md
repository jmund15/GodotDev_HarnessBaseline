# Sizing a review panel

Read before seating a fixed review panel (`orchestration` §2 *Sizing the width* points here).

A fixed review panel has three settings. **Coverage** is the command's mandates, and each review stage covers what only its input can show. A review before execution judges decisions: scope, missed consumers, premises, grounding, test readiness and doctrine conflicts. A review after execution judges what only the result shows (bugs, gaps, divergence from the approved plan) and the shipped text. Proposed literal wording is judged once, at close: its first round reviews each shipped text hunk; later rounds review only hunks changed since that read. A mandate that judges what another stage's mandate already judges is a coverage defect: rescope one of them. **Width** is the seat count: one seat per admitted mandate, unless the size or room rule below merges seats. Width buys recall. A supplementary seat (ladder §Role definitions) is the one seat beyond that count: an added reader on mandates a qualified seat already carries. It adds recall and no mandate, and covers none alone. **Depth** is model and effort per seat, set by how deep defects can hide, never by seat count; the ladder names the review tier and effort.

Size rule: where the caller sets a rigor (thorough, normal or light), it sets what a first round must catch, never its seat count. Thorough covers every admitted mandate, the full recall the owner chose; a merge keeps each mandate covered. Below thorough, light holds the bar at defects that would force rework or a wrong result, and normal adds significant gaps; size the seats from the subject against that bar (`orchestration` §0 *Size every dispatch from its work*): merge mandates one reader can judge in one pass at the depth the bar needs, and give a mandate its own seat when the subject outgrows one reader's context or its risk needs a reader of its own. Merges follow the command's seat map order. Report the rigor, the seats and why. A caller that sets its own width for a stage uses that width.

Room rule: before a panel, read the room for its seat tier (`provider_bands.py room <tier> --json`). When room is low (the drive's Conditions Room rule), narrow the panel by merging what one reader can still judge at the bar, or wait for room, never dropping below the bar; report the room reading and the choice. A tier never lowers a command's own trigger. An always-loaded rule change adds no seat: the instruction-quality seat audits it. Subjects reviewed together share one map; seats never multiply per subject.

A merged seat keeps per-mandate coverage: `checked.mandates` gives every mandate it carried one token (findings, clean or not reached), `checked.basis` the evidence behind each clean, and each finding carries its mandate key. Key a catalog seat by its lens names joined with `+`. `review_ledger.py record` refuses a rostered seat with no mandate outcomes. A bespoke seat can record explicit valid mandate outcomes. Report a not-reached mandate as UNCOVERED. Record one verdict row per seat with its `mandates` block (`/orchestration_metrics` *Incremental rating*).

Run `tools/review_ledger.py plan` before seating a panel and seat only the mandates it returns; they are a ceiling, and a later round's width comes from [Later review rounds](later_rounds.md). When `plan` reports `crossFamily.required`, seat one candidate it lists from a family the covering rows did not use, even when the ordinary roster comes back empty; only an exact-revision reuse (`crossFamily.reusedBy`) clears it. If no such family has room, use a different model of the same family and record it with `--degraded`.
