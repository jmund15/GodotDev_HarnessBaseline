# A job's rows and window

Read before leaving a cheaper row out of a job's rows, or before pinning a larger window (`orchestration` Pre-Dispatch Checklist points here).

## Which rows a job lists

A job's rows leave out a cheaper row that clears the ladder's floor only for a named reason: quality. Under lean the reason is the `±` clause that bars it, never quality alone. A `±` risk bars the cheaper row only when that failure would cost this job more than the price gap: a final report the commits and diff can rebuild costs one read. A shape that cannot say why it beats the cheaper one takes the cheaper one. Rows come from `for-role`'s cells for this job's work, never from a model or effort a plan or an earlier jobs file named. A check-scored job records its `spec` and omits its effort (§5 *Effort*); other work takes the lowest effort in its cell that meets the quality floor for its depth, and its effort fit is rated after the result.

## A larger window

Pin a larger window (`-M`) only when the work needs everything resident at once, and state its price: a larger window can bill at a long-context rate. Set `window: "full"` only where `reference/model_ladder_evidence.md` *Compaction* allows it.
