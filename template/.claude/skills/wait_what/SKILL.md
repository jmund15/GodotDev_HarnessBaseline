---
name: wait_what
description: >-
  Load when the user signals the previous answer lost them — "wait, what?", "huh?",
  "I don't follow", "you've lost me", or any equivalent that reports comprehension
  failed rather than asking a new question. SKIP when the user is disputing the
  content (that is an objection — diagnose it) or asking a genuine follow-up question.
---

The signal names the listener's state, not your output length: comprehension failed
here. Do NOT defend the previous answer, repeat it louder, or expand it — and do NOT
just cut words, which produces a telegram and loses them further.

Re-pitch the same content one altitude higher: the one-sentence version first, then
at most three load-bearing points, each in the project's own vocabulary (`game_vision`
for design/scope terms, `pp_subsystems` for system and ownership names).

You decide how far back to go — the lost thread may start well before the last message,
so re-pitch from the premise you skipped, not from the last paragraph.
