# A4 — carried and dropped: `/plan`, `/drive` and the commands they absorb

Rows for every phase of the five merged commands (`/design_drive`, `/feature_drive`, `/part_execute`, `/plan_handoff`, `/part_drive`) and of the five shared files they ran on (`drive_spine.md`, `drive_execution.md`, `drive_close.md`, `execution_depth.md`, `rigor_stages.md`), all deleted in A4's commit. Paths are below `template/.claude/`. "Carried" names the v2 line that states the same decision in its own sentence. "Dropped" says what dropping it does for later efficiency and flexibility (E2); no row rests on a usage count.

Source bytes: the lane's `v2` copies, which carry MaddManager's latest publish (`148efbd`). PushinPotions' `origin/main` copies of `feature_drive.md`, `part_drive.md`, `drive_*.md`, `execution_depth.md` and `rigor_stages.md` are older (they lack the UI-skill and mode lines), so the lane bytes are the superset.
