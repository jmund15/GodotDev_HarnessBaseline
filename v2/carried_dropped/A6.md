# Carried/dropped: A6 (review-step rewires)

A6 moves the review step of `/review_pr`, `/pr_ready` and `/architecture_brainstorm_redteam` onto `/review`'s seat route: `/review` §1 to §3 (the brief, one seat or two, the `quote_check.py` claim check), with each command's own lens file as the checklist. `/idea_brainstorm_fanout` and `/doc_architecture_audit` keep their engines (plan §Capabilities, `idea_fanout.js` and `doc_architecture_audit.js` rows) and lose their citations of deleted tools. A6 removed no always-loaded rule, so every row here is a phase or rule of a rewritten command. **Carried** names where it went. **Dropped** says what dropping it does for later efficiency and flexibility (E2).

Sources: the shared v1 bytes of `commands/review_pr.md`, `pr_ready.md`, `architecture_brainstorm_redteam.md`, `agents/redteam_agents.md`, `idea_brainstorm_fanout.md` and `doc_architecture_audit.md`, plus PushinPotions' forked `review_pr.md` and `pr_ready.md` at `origin/main` (OF-forked: the rewrite is the reconcile). `commands/reference/doc_architecture_audit_report.md` is unchanged.
